Terms and Condition of Employment Agreement
1. Date of Joining – Your date of join 2. Salary Details Your monthly gross salary including all benefits will be 12,000/-. Income Tax, Professional Tax and other taxes, if any, wherever applicable will be deducted at source from your monthly compensation as per the government rules and regulations. Revision of salary will be reviewed periodically as per the policy of the Company. Revision will be on the basis of demonstrated results and effectiveness of performance during the period of review. 3. Office working days & Timing The firm commercial operation includes working 6 days in a week. The working hours are as below: Team B - Monday to Friday (09.30 a.m. to 7.00 p.m.) Saturday – 1st & 3rd & 5th as above Saturday - 2nd and 4th are usually off You shall be working as part of Team and you need to adhere to the same at all-time till further instruction. Half Day calculation – a) If start time is after 12 p.m. then the day will be calculated as half day. b) If end time is before 4.30 pm then the day will be calculated as half day. c) If the start time is after 12 pm and end-time is before 4.30 pm then then it will be considered as absent for that day unless there is genuine reason which is approved by their manager or partner, which shall be conveyed to HR. d) Saturday working – For the staff who are working at in Audit team having 2nd & 4th Saturday off,may need to work on those Saturday which is off, in case the work demands and Audit in- charge/partner ask them to work. No extra salary or compensatory off will be given for those days. e) Sunday/Holiday working If the staff is working on Sunday or any declared holiday then extra salary will be paid or can be compensated against the leaves taken during the month. This is subject to approval of the partner for their respective teams. 4. Leave Policy and Holiday You will be entitled to Festivals & National Holidays as per the Holiday Calendar declared by HR every year. Privileged leaves will be given as per the leave policy of the Firm. For the purposes of the leave the year shall run from 1st February to 31st January. You are entitled for the Paid leave of 24 days per year or on a pro-rata bases (which takes into account from the date of joining) once the probation is completed after continuous service of 6 months from the date of joining. Leaves should be applied for in advance and should be approved by the concerned official. The Firm reserves the right to grant/deny the leave applied, depending on the contingency of work. Any Holiday or Sunday falling in between the continuous leave will be considered as leave.The accumulated PL as on 31st January of every year will be enchased as per leave encashment policy of the firm. However, the leave encashment will not be permitted in the case if you quit the services without requisite notice or whose services are terminated on account of misconduct, such as, dishonesty, gross negligence etc. 5. Job and responsibilities You shall, to the best of your ability, work for the Firm on all matters relating to your profile and other related activities that may be assigned to you by the Firm from time to time. You are expected to render your Job Responsibilities with utmost dedication and honesty. 6. Attire All staff needs to come in formal attire from Monday to Thursday compulsory in office or at client place.
